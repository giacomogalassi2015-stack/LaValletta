Frequently Asked Questions (F.A.Q.)

Everything you need to know for your stay in Riomaggiore

Arrival & Transport

How do I get to Riomaggiore by car?
Via the A12 motorway (La Spezia exit). The village is a ZTL (Restricted Traffic Zone): you can only reach the village entrance by car.
Where can I park in Riomaggiore?
At the covered 'Rio Park' garage (approx. €35/day) or along limited stretches of the coastal road, but spaces are scarce.
Why do you recommend leaving the car in La Spezia?
La Spezia offers more parking options (most of them paid) and is only an 8-minute train ride from Riomaggiore.
Is the train really the best option?
Yes, the 'Cinque Terre Express' is fast, frequent (every 15–20 min in high season) and connects all the villages stress-free. Ticket prices vary by season.
When is the Cinque Terre Card worth it?
The 'Trekking Card' is worth it for paid trails like the Sentiero Azzurro. The 'Train Card' pays off if you plan to take more than three train trips per day.
How do the ferries work?
They operate from late March to October. The views are spectacular, but services are cancelled when the sea is rough.

Staying at Ca' della Valletta

Is the property far from the train station?
We are about 100 metres from the station — a flat 5-minute walk through the pedestrian tunnel.
Are there many stairs to reach you?
Riomaggiore is a vertical village. We have a short flight of ten steps at the entrance and internal ramps to reach the rooms (1st and 2nd floor). We recommend backpacks or medium-sized trolleys rather than large suitcases.
Do you offer luggage storage?
We don't have internal luggage storage. Early drop-off in the room is possible by prior arrangement. There are two paid luggage storage services at the station and a few metres from us.
What is the Wi-Fi and air conditioning like?
Wi-Fi is free, fast and covers the whole property. Each room has its own independent air conditioning unit that guests can adjust themselves.
What amenities will I find in the room?
Towels, hairdryer, kettle (tea/coffee), mini-fridge, safe and a personal care toiletry set.

Experiences & Food

Is the Via dell'Amore open?
Yes, it reopened in 2024. A trekking card is required (available online or at info points). We recommend visiting early in the morning.
Should I book restaurants in advance?
Yes — in high season the best spots fill up days in advance.
Which hiking trails do you recommend from Riomaggiore?
Besides the Via dell'Amore, the trail to the Sanctuary of Montenero offers the most beautiful view of the entire coastline.
What can I do if it rains?
Trains keep running. You can visit the castles, historic churches or local museums in the various villages.
