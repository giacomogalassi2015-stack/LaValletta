Cookie Policy

Effective from 28/01/2026

1. What are Cookies

Cookies are small text files that sites visited by users send to their terminals, where they are stored to be re-transmitted to the same sites on subsequent visits. Cookies are used for various purposes, have different characteristics, and can be used both by the owner of the site you are visiting and by third parties.

Below you will find all the information on cookies installed through this site and the necessary instructions on how to manage your preferences regarding them.

2. Cookies used by this site

The data controller is Giacomo Galassi, owner of the site www.cadellavalletta.it.

A. Technical Cookies (Do not require consent)

These cookies are essential for the proper functioning of the site. They include:

  • Navigation or session cookies (to guarantee normal navigation and use of the website).
  • Functionality cookies (allow the user to navigate according to selected criteria, such as language, in order to improve the service provided).
  • Quote tool: the booking quote tool available on room pages processes the selected dates exclusively within the user's browser to calculate the stay cost and deposit amount. No personal data is transmitted to or stored on any server as a result of this calculation.

B. Third Party Services and Cookies

This site integrates external services to function correctly. Details below:

  • Google Sheets: Used to securely fetch dynamic pricing data. (Privacy Policy: Google).
  • Teamup: Used to fetch and display calendar availability. (Privacy Policy: Teamup).

3. Management of cookie preferences

Upon first access, via the banner, the user can consent to the use of cookies. Furthermore, it is possible to manage preferences directly through the browser.

If you do not know the type and version of the browser you are using, click on "Help" in the browser window at the top, from which you can access all the necessary information. If you know your browser, click on the one you are using to access the cookie management page:

  • Google Chrome
  • Mozilla Firefox
  • Apple Safari
  • Microsoft Edge / IE

4. Rights of the data subject

At any time you may exercise your rights towards the Data Controller, pursuant to EU Regulation 2016/679 (GDPR). For more information on your rights and how your data is processed, we invite you to consult our Privacy Policy.
