Frequently Asked Questions (F.A.Q.)

Everything you need to know for your stay in Riomaggiore

Arrival & Transport

How do I get to Riomaggiore by car?
Via the A12 motorway (La Spezia exit). The village is a ZTL (Restricted Zone): you can only reach the village entrance.
Where can I park in Riomaggiore?
At the 'Rio Park' garage (approx. 35€/day) or limited spots along the coastal road.
Why leave the car in La Spezia?
More parking options and only an 8-minute train ride to Riomaggiore.
Is the train the best way?
Yes, the 'Cinque Terre Express' is fast, frequent, and stress-free. Prices vary by season.
Which Cinque Terre Card should I buy?
The 'Trekking Card' is for paths. The 'Train Card' is best for 3+ train trips per day.
How do the ferries work?
Late March to October. Spectacular views, but cancelled if the sea is rough.

Staying at Ca' della Valletta

Distance from the station?
About 100 meters, a flat 5-minute walk through the tunnel.
Are there many stairs?
We have 10 steps at the entrance and internal ramps. Backpacks are highly recommended.
Do you have luggage storage?
Not internally. Room drop-off possible early by agreement. Paid storage available at the station and nearby.
Wi-Fi and AC?
Free fast Wi-Fi and independent AC in every room.
Room amenities?
Towels, hairdryer, kettle, mini-fridge, safe, and toiletries.

Experiences & Food

Is Via dell'Amore open?
Yes (2024). Trekking card required. Best visited early morning.
Restaurant bookings?
Highly recommended days in advance during high season.
Best hiking trails?
The path to the Sanctuary of Montenero offers the best coastal views.
What if it rains?
Trains still run. Visit castles, historic churches, or local museums.
